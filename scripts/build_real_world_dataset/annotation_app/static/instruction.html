Instruction

Welcome to the flood-depth annotation demo. Please read the instructions carefully before proceeding.

Objective

You will be shown images of vehicles partially submerged in floodwaters. Your task is to estimate the flood depth by selecting a matching car type and adjusting a 3D model.

Steps

  1. Select a car type:
    Based on the image on the left, choose the car category on the right (e.g., SUV, Compact Car) that most closely resembles the vehicle in the flood scene. Then click "Select 3D car model".
  2. Adjust the water level:
    A 3D model of the selected car will appear next to the flood image. Use your mouse to rotate (left-click + drag) and zoom (scroll wheel) the view. Adjust the flood depth using the slider until the blue water cube matches the waterline seen in the image. (You can either use the mouse or keyboard ← and → to control the slider.) Then click “Next Image”.

    3. Hints

    • If the car type doesn't match, use "Change Car" to pick another.
    • For images with multiple cars, select the car that is unobstructed and closest to the foreground.
    • If the car is tilted or not level, use the midpoint of the visible waterline as your reference for flood depth.
    • If you are unsure, provide your best estimate.

    Important

    Your responses will help improve flood risk modeling. Please take your time and do your best.
